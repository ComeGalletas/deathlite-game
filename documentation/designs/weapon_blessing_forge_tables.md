# Weapon blessings, Forgings and upgrades

Generated by `tools/gen_weapon_tables.py` from `data/weapons/*.json` and `data/heroes/meta_upgrades.json` on 2026-09-19; rerun it after a tuning pass. Blessing values are the **cumulative total at that level** (the catalog stores totals; applying level N adds the difference to N-1). Every blessing has five levels. The `→` lines show the weapon's own number before and after each level, using the base weapon (or the forged weapon for a post-Forge blessing).

Tables regenerated and every claim re-verified against the data and the engine on **2026-09-20**, when the six-blessings rebuild was merged and the weapon kind weight raised to 10. The sections that are *not* generated — "Not in the generated findings", "Where a blessing can come from", "Heroes", "Nominal output per weapon" and "Multipliers outside the offering" — were written by hand in that pass.

> **Drift warning.** The header and "Review findings" are emitted by `tools/gen_weapon_tables.py`, not stored here; the hand-written sections are not in the generator at all. Rerunning the generator overwrites the findings with its own copy and drops every hand-written section. Fold anything worth keeping into the generator's `bullets([...])` block before the next rerun.

## Review findings

What stood out while building the tables. Numbers come from the data files; balance and consistency notes; the open defect the 2026-09-12 review found is fixed.

- **The three Forge cards that say the blow is lighter now are (2026-09-19).** The 2026-09-12 tuning pass moved Bomb damage 28 → 23 and Hammer 25 → 27 without revisiting `data/weapons/forges.json`, so Meteor Hammer (27), Cluster Bomb (24) and Minefield (26) all hit as hard as or harder than the base while their cards said lighter. The overrides are re-based on the current weapons: Meteor Hammer 23 (0.85 of the Hammer's 27; the crater's 0.35 dps × 2.5 s is worth most of a blow), Cluster Bomb 20 and Minefield 21 (0.86 and 0.93 of the Bomb's 23, the ratios the Forgings shipped with).
- **Six own blessings per weapon (2026-09-19).** Every weapon, summons included, carries one damage blessing, one speed / frequency blessing, one or two coverage blessings and the rest special effects; the six cross-weapon synergies and the post-Forge blessings sit on top and stay gated. Damage blessings follow `base × (1.15^n − 1)` so level V doubles the weapon and every step is 10-20 % over the previous level; speed blessings are ×0.87 / 0.76 / 0.66 / 0.57 / 0.50; the trade cards (Heavy Blade, Heavy Draw) are sized so the net output still climbs ×1.15 a level. Coverage grows about +20-25 % hit area a level. Blast Amplifier is gone. See `journals/six_blessings_journal.md`.
- **Two coverage cards multiply hits and sit above the yardstick.** Arcane Missiles (+3 bolts at V) and More Embers (+5 embers at V) scale output close to linearly against a single target. Both are identity cards from the design and were kept as they were.
- **Cooldown penalties are flat.** Heavy Blade and Heavy Draw multiply cooldown by ×1.15 at every level (the level-to-level delta is ×1.0), so the penalty is paid in full at level I and the later levels are pure gain. Intended, but worth knowing when reading the tables.
- **Stacking that reaches the floor.** Quick Hands V halves the Daggers cooldown to 0.2 s; with Haste V (÷1.32) that is 0.15 s and with Flurry V at five stacks (÷1.6) 0.095 s, still above the 0.05 s floor. Rapid Draw V with Heavy Draw gives the Bow 1.2 × 0.5 × 1.15 = 0.69 s. Dense Field V on Minefield lays a mine every 0.65 s with a 25 s lifetime, so up to about 38 live mines at once.
- **Constant effect rows.** Executioner's threshold (35% HP), Hunter's Mark's and Flurry's caps (5 stacks), Overcharge's cadence (every 4th cast), Chain's range (200 px), Sticky Bomb's flag and the on-hit status durations are stored as five equal levels. They print correctly and never change; the tables show them once.
- **Name collisions between systems.** Fortune and Scholar exist as both a meta upgrade and a blessing, Swiftness is a meta upgrade and an item affix, and Vitality / Haste / Fortune are both blessings and affixes. Nothing breaks, but the run-status screen lists them side by side.
- **All effect keys are wired.** Every `weapon_effect` key and Forge effect key in the data is read by code (combat.py, synergy.py, core.py, slam.py, bomb.py, effects.py, state.py), and the item affix tags `area` and `elite` are honoured by the tag-damage aggregate. The on-hit status keys are read by suffix (`_on_hit_frac`, `_on_hit_potency`, `_on_hit_duration`).

### Not in the generated findings

Two defects the generator does not emit, both left over from the 2026-09-16 Daggers retune (`3cf7e6f`) and untouched by the six-blessings pass, which re-based the *Hammer* and *Bomb* Forge overrides but not the Daggers':

- **Both Daggers Forgings are downgrades.** The retune took the Daggers from 5 dmg / 0.42 s / area 22 / cone ±23° to **9 / 0.4 / 42 / ±17°**, and `forges.json` was not revisited. Twin Daggers still overrides damage to 4 — that was 0.8 of the base blade, it is now 0.44, and the fanned pair lands 20 dps against the unforged weapon's 22.5, so the Forging loses even when both blades connect. Fan of Blades overrides to 5 at a 0.5 s cooldown: 10 dps on one target, 44 % of the unforged weapon, and it gives up the pierce too. Re-basing on the ratios they shipped with gives about **7** for Twin Daggers and **8** for Fan of Blades.
- **Fan of Blades inherited the area buff as a projectile radius.** `area` is the cone's reach for a melee weapon and the projectile's hit radius for a projectile one (`combat/weapons/core.py:507`). Fan of Blades overrides `category` melee → projectile but leaves `area` alone, so each thrown dagger's hit radius went 22 → 42 — the Bomb's blast radius, against the Bow's 5 and the Rod's 6. Almost certainly not intended by a melee retune; if it was, the card should say so.

## Offering rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Cards per level-up | 3 |
| Kind weights (stat / weapon / grant / forge) | 10 / 10 / 10 / 8 |
| Rarity weights (common / uncommon / rare / forge) | 1.0 / 0.55 / 0.28 / 0.08 |
| Level falloff (weight × at level I..V) | 1 / 0.85 / 0.7 / 0.55 / 0.4 |
| Summon grant weight factor | 0.6 |
| Blessing levels a weapon needs before it can be forged | 2 |
| Weapon slots (melee + ranged) / summon slots | 3 / 1 |
| Forgings per weapon | 1 of 2, exclusive and permanent |

### Where a blessing can come from

The "Offering rules" table above is the level-up. Three paths hand out the same cards, all of them through `progression/blessings/offer.py`, so every gate below applies to all three.

| Path | Cards shown | Kinds allowed | Rarity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level-up (`roll_offering`) | 3, the hero picks one | stat, weapon, grant, forge | the rarity weights above |
| Chest (`core/chests.py:_grant_blessing`) | 1, applied straight away | stat, weapon — never a weapon grant, never a Forge | rolled from the chest's tier, then any rarity as a fallback |
| Shrine (`core/locations.py:grant_random_blessing`) | 1, applied straight away | stat, weapon | unconstrained; heals 30 HP instead if the offering is empty |

Chest blessing odds by chest tier: common none, uncommon 35 % for a common card, rare always (65 % common / 35 % uncommon), epic always (65 % uncommon / 35 % rare).

Gates that hold on every path: a post-Forge card (`requires.forge`) needs one of the hero's weapons to carry that Forge; a synergy card (`requires.weapons`) needs the named second weapon in hand; a Forge card needs the weapon to hold `forge_requires_levels` = 2 blessing levels, and a weapon can be forged once, permanently. Weapon slots cap at `MAX_WEAPONS` = 3 melee-plus-ranged and one summon (`progression/upgrades.py`).

## Multipliers outside the offering

Three systems change weapon output at run time without appearing anywhere in the tables above.

- **Buff buildings** (`data/world/buildings.json`, 2 – 5 per island, one use each, 10 s): **Haste** adds +1.0 to `attack_speed_multiplier`, which halves every weapon's and summon's cooldown for its duration — the single largest multiplier in the game, and the one that takes a Quick-Hands-V Daggers to a 0.10 s cooldown. **Vampire** adds +0.10 `damage_multiplier` and heals 1 HP per enemy hit by any weapon. **Turbo** (+150 speed, +20 armour, 5 dps contact damage), **Magnet** and **Pinball** (a 40-damage bouncing projectile every 2 s) do not scale weapon damage.
- **Meta upgrades** (Salvage shop, below): Ferocity is a flat +1 % `damage_multiplier` per level, +10 % at max, applied before any blessing.
- **Equipment** (below): `damage_multiplier`, `attack_speed_multiplier`, `area_multiplier`, `crit_chance`, `crit_damage` and `projectile_speed_multiplier` affixes all land on the same hero stats the blessings feed, and `of the Maw` / `of the Hunt` add damage against the `area` and `elite` tags.

## Where the six blessings came from

The six-blessings rebuild landed on `main` on **2026-09-20**. It gave every weapon six blessings of its own across four axes, sized every level to a compounding ×1.15 so level V doubles the weapon, removed Blast Amplifier, re-tagged Weak Point and Demolitionist as `behavior`, re-based the three Forge overrides that claimed a lighter blow (Meteor Hammer 23, Cluster Bomb 20, Minefield 21), and added eighteen cards with the engine hooks they need. The catalog went from 71 blessings to 88.

The weapon kind weight went to **10** in the same pass, parity with `stat` and `grant`, to pay for the six cards a weapon now owns. That deliberately retires the 2026-09-09 rule that a stat card shows in practically every level-up: measured over 600 offerings with three weapons held, a stat card now appears in about 87 % of level-ups against 93 % at weight 7, and a weapon card in about 84 % against 75 %.

The full reasoning, the measured per-level ladders against the training dummy and the two corrections the measurement forced (the Hammer's speed card sized on the whole swing-plus-rest cycle, the Ember Ring's spinning the ring rather than shortening a rehit that never binds) are in `documentation/journals/six_blessings_journal.md`.
