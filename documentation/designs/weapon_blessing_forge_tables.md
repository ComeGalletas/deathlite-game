# Weapon blessings, Forgings and upgrades

Generated by `tools/gen_weapon_tables.py` from `data/weapons/*.json` and `data/heroes/meta_upgrades.json` on 2026-09-12; rerun it after a tuning pass. Blessing values are the **cumulative total at that level** (the catalog stores totals; applying level N adds the difference to N-1). Every blessing has five levels. The `→` lines show the weapon's own number before and after each level, using the base weapon (or the forged weapon for a post-Forge blessing).

## Review findings

What stood out while building the tables. Numbers come from the data files; the first three are real defects, the rest are balance and consistency notes.

- **Three Forge cards say the blow is lighter, but the numbers no longer are.** The 2026-09-12 tuning pass changed Bomb damage 28 → 23 and Hammer 25 → 27 in `data/weapons/weapons.json`, and `data/weapons/forges.json` was not revisited. Meteor Hammer overrides damage to 27, equal to the new base, while its card says "the blow itself lands lighter". Cluster Bomb overrides to 24 and Minefield to 26, both above the new base of 23, while their cards say "the first blast is lighter" and "a little lighter". Either lower the overrides (about 22 / 18 / 20 keeps the old ratios) or drop the claims from the card text.
- **The Bomb carries two coverage blessings that do the same job and no behavior blessing.** Bigger Explosion adds up to +60 blast radius and Blast Amplifier multiplies by up to ×1.75; together they take the base 42 px blast to about 178 px before the hero's area multiplier. Every other main weapon has one coverage blessing and at least one behavior blessing.
- **Blessing counts are uneven across weapons.** Sword has 5 own blessings plus 1 synergy, Hammer 5, Bow 5 plus 2 synergy, Daggers 4 plus 2 synergy, Magic Rod 4, Bomb 4 plus 1 synergy, Ember Ring 3, Grave Totem 4, Spirit Wolf 3. The Magic Rod has no synergy card of its own; it is only the trigger for Marked Prey and Crossfire on other weapons.
- **Cooldown penalties are flat.** Heavy Blade and Heavy Draw multiply cooldown by ×1.15 at every level (the level-to-level delta is ×1.0), so the penalty is paid in full at level I and the later levels are pure gain. Intended, but worth knowing when reading the tables.
- **Stacking that reaches the floor.** Quick Hands V halves the Daggers cooldown to 0.21 s; with Haste V (÷1.32) that is 0.16 s, still above the 0.05 s floor. Rapid Draw V with Heavy Draw gives the Bow 1.2 × 0.55 × 1.15 = 0.76 s. Dense Field V on Minefield lays a mine every 0.65 s with a 25 s lifetime, so up to about 38 live mines at once.
- **Constant effect rows.** Executioner's threshold (35% HP), Hunter's Mark's cap (5 stacks), Overcharge's cadence (every 5th cast) and Chain's range (200 px) are stored as five equal levels. They print correctly and never change; the tables show them once.
- **Name collisions between systems.** Fortune and Scholar exist as both a meta upgrade and a blessing, Swiftness is a meta upgrade and an item affix, and Vitality / Haste / Fortune are both blessings and affixes. Nothing breaks, but the run-status screen lists them side by side.
- **All effect keys are wired.** Every `weapon_effect` key and Forge effect key in the data is read by code (combat.py, synergy.py, core.py, slam.py, bomb.py, effects.py), and the item affix tags `area` and `elite` are honoured by the tag-damage aggregate.

## Offering rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Cards per level-up | 3 |
| Kind weights (stat / weapon / grant / forge) | 10 / 7 / 10 / 8 |
| Rarity weights (common / uncommon / rare / forge) | 1.0 / 0.55 / 0.28 / 0.08 |
| Level falloff (weight × at level I..V) | 1 / 0.85 / 0.7 / 0.55 / 0.4 |
| Summon grant weight factor | 0.6 |
| Blessing levels a weapon needs before it can be forged | 2 |
| Weapon slots (melee + ranged) / summon slots | 3 / 1 |
| Forgings per weapon | 1 of 2, exclusive and permanent |

## Equipment (random items)

An item is one base stat plus 0/1/2/3/4 affixes for common/uncommon/rare/epic/legendary, plus the slot's unique effect at legendary. Values scale by `1 + 0.05 × (item level − 1)`.
